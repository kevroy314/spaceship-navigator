\section{Where a coarse representation breaks}
\label{app:equalpull}

Where in a level a coarse representation breaks is governed by
instantaneous force competition rather than by the field's resonance
structure, although the latter is the candidate the literature on
constructed chaos suggests \cite{chirikov1979,wisdom1980,deck2013}, since
resonance overlap describes what happens over many orbital periods while an
episode here is a fraction of one (placing targets in the Wisdom band raises
$\eta$ only from $0.0026$ to $0.0172$). The relevant surface for an episode
this short is the one on which a secondary's pull equals its primary's, at
a distance $d = a\sqrt{m/M}$ from a secondary of mass $m$ orbiting a primary
of mass $M$ at semi-major axis $a$ (about half a Hill radius, and distinct
from the Laplace sphere of influence of patched-conic design
\cite{bate1971}). Figure~\ref{fig:eta}(a) shows $\eta$ on shells around the
second-most-massive body of 297 levels from nine families, as a median over
24 directions at each radius: it peaks at $0.43$ at $1.0\times$ the
equal-pull radius and falls to $0.020$ at $8\times$, and the generator
places targets in contested levels in the $0.8$--$1.3\times$ shell. The
larger lever by far is family choice (Figure~\ref{fig:eta}(b)): median
$\eta$ over the playable area spans three orders of magnitude, from
\code{debris\_maze} ($0.0005$) to \code{star\_cluster} ($0.53$).
