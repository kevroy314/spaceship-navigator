The four tracks say different things

Same policy, same training run, four missions from the frozen evaluation suites. The spread between them is wider than the spread between any two training configurations measured so far.

What you are looking at

  • These are sampled rollouts, not the greedy policy. The frozen-suite evaluation takes the argmax of the policy's logits and reports one number per checkpoint. Here the policy samples its actions, which is why easy task 0 arrives 52% of the time while the greedy agent scores 89% on the easy suite. The point is the spread the single number hides.
  • Grey discs are bodies, with their orbits traced faintly. The ring is the active waypoint, drawn where it sits over the episode — on most of these missions the target is itself in motion.
  • A line stopping early means the attempt ended there, by arrival, collision, fuel or hazard. Only timeouts run the full 150 seconds.
  • In replay, all 496 fly on one shared mission clock, so the fleet thins as attempts end. A finished attempt leaves a dot in its outcome colour — bright for an arrival, faint for everything else — and the green ring tracks the waypoint, which on these missions is itself orbiting. One second of wall clock is one second of mission time at 1×.
  • One run, one seed. This is abl_info_s0, trained with eta,stretch and rolled out the same way. It is one of five seeds behind that configuration, not a summary of them.
  • Hard task 0 never arrives — 0 of 496. The planner lands only about 16% of two-stop tours at this clock, so part of that is the tier and not the agent.
