What Each Level Forces

The continuous difficulty space, cut into named skills. Each lesson is defined by something measured about its solutions, not by the generator that made it — and each comes with the cockpit instrument that makes its lesson legible.

The left panel of each lesson is its causal map: every single burn the ship could make, as when (horizontal) against which way (vertical), brightness being the fraction of that cell that arrives. Slide the delta-v above to move through the volume and watch regions appear, widen and merge. A broad patch means many sufficient options. A thin sliver means one necessary action. Separate patches are genuinely different strategies, and a diagonal stripe is leading: burn later, aim further ahead.

Cells hold a win fraction over jittered samples rather than a yes or no. An n-body basin boundary can be fractal, and a binary grid would report its own spacing as structure. The right panel pairs the per-decision causal effect — randomise one segment of a working plan, see how often it still arrives — with the boundary's uncertainty exponent.

Read the exponent with its caption, not on its own. α is fitted to how often a perturbation of size ε flips the outcome, and it is only a measurement in a middle band: if the flip rate at the smallest ε already sits at the independence ceiling 2p(1−p) — the rate at which two unrelated parameter points disagree — the slope is sampling noise dressed as structure, and if almost nothing flips at all the fit is reading a handful of binomial counts. Each card states the flip counts behind its own α and says which of the two is happening.
